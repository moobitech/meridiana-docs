## Community

Free, for the Angular and React views, for:

- individuals, for non-commercial projects;
- open-source projects under an OSI-approved license;
- organizations with less than €1M of annual revenue and at most 5 developers working with
  Meridiana.

You still need a key, free: request it for your application with the button above. When your
organization grows past these limits, move to the Web plan. React Native and the resource timeline
are not part of the Community plan.

## One application, every environment

A license covers **one application**: its name, its environments (development, staging,
production), its tenants and its web and mobile editions. The key lists its hosts
(`app.example.com`, or `*.example.com` for the domain and its subdomains) and its bundle ids
(`com.example.app`), and works only there. `localhost` and Expo Go always work, for development.
Another application needs its own license; a new host for the same application needs only a new key,
free.

## What every license includes

- **A perpetual license for the versions you got.** Every version released during your
  subscription stays licensed forever, even if you do not renew: your application never breaks.
- **Updates** for the whole subscription, and **support** with the paid plans.
- **Unlimited developers and end users**, with no royalties.
- **No server**: the license key is checked offline, on the device.

## Renewals

Renewing is cheaper than buying again:

| When you renew | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Before the end of your subscription | **50% off** the list price |
| Within 60 days after it ended | **25% off** the list price |
| Later | the list price, as a new license |

A discounted renewal continues from the end of your previous subscription: the versions released in
between are covered, and you receive a new key with the later end date. You can change the plan
when you renew.

## How to buy

1. Click the button of your plan: it opens an email with the details we need, including the hosts
   and bundle ids of your application.
2. We reply with the order and the payment details (none for Community).
3. You receive the license key. Pass it to the provider, as [Licensing](licensing.md) explains:
   the watermark disappears.

Not sure yet? Install Meridiana from npm and evaluate it for 30 days in a non-production
environment, no key needed. Enterprise covers many applications, custom terms and the redistribution
cases that the standard license does not allow (see [`LICENSE.md`](../LICENSE.md), section 5).
